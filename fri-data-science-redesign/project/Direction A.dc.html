Master's programme

Master of Computer and Data Science

Two years, four semesters, 120 ECTS. An intensive programme built on theoretical foundations, practical method, and hands-on work with real data — for students with a technical or mathematical background who intend to lead in the field, in industry or in research.

The programme sits inside the second-cycle Computer and Information Science degree at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science, University of Ljubljana. Enrolment is open to anyone who has completed a first-cycle programme with sufficient technical and mathematical content.
