Master's programme

Master of Computer and Data Science

Two years, four semesters, 120 ECTS — theoretical foundations, practical method, and hands-on work with real data. Built for students with a technical or mathematical background who intend to lead in the field, in industry or in research.

The programme sits inside the second-cycle Computer and Information Science degree at UL FRI. Entry is open to anyone with a completed first-cycle programme carrying sufficient technical and mathematical content. Teaching is in English.
