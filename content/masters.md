The programme is part of the second-cycle master's study programme Computer and Information Science at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science, University of Ljubljana. Upon completion, graduates earn the title Master of Computer and Data Science.

It is designed for two years of full-time study, divided into four semesters of 30 ECTS each, for a total of 120 ECTS. Enrolment is open to anyone who has completed a first-cycle programme, or an equivalent study programme, with sufficient technical and mathematical content.
